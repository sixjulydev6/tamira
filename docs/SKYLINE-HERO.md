`src/story/skyline/` adapts the supplied layout, building/room data, architectural scan shaders, LiDAR extraction, wiring geometry, ground shaders and camera paths to ordinary Three.js factories. `index.js` owns loading, atmosphere, composer, lifecycle and the bridge. `scene.js` retains the existing product choreography and finale, and no longer imports the old `city.js`/`daylight.js` opener.
